# IC2+

IC2+ is a Minecraft mod for **1.12.2 Forge** that expands **IndustrialCraft 2** with new nuclear technologies,
materials, reactor components and advanced equipment.

I don't know when the mod will be finished, as it is developed in my free time, but here is what is already implemented:

## Features

### Nuclear Materials

- Thorium
- Uranium-233
- Plutonium
- Americium
- Curium
- Californium
- Intalium
- Oganesson
- Unbiquadium-310

### Reactor Improvements

- New nuclear fuel rods
- Elite Heat Vent
- Ultimate Heat Vent
- Super Cooling Rod

### New Content

- Ametrine ore and shards
- Thorium ore
- Strange Quark Block
- Neutron Paste

### End-game Technology

- Neutron Star Armor
- Neutron Star entity
- Energy beam system

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.12.2
- Forge
- IndustrialCraft 2

The mod is still under development. Features may change in the future.
